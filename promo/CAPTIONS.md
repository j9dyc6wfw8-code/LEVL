the words should get out of the way. Captions 1 and 2 suit **Cut A (product)**,
where the viewer is already reading.

## Posting notes

- **Attach a trending sound in the composer.** The MP4 ships silent on purpose —
  in-app audio gets credited and picked up by that sound's discovery feed;
  burnt-in music doesn't.
- **Cover frame:** Cut A — ~26.5s (end card) or ~2.2s (logo lockup). Cut B —
  ~23.5s (end card) or ~0.95s (the icon slam with the shockwave rings); a
  pre-made still for each is in `cover.jpg` / `cover-hype.jpg`. Avoid mid-scene
  frames, they're mid-motion.
- **Cut B flashes.** It uses white strobe frames on the heavy hits. That's fine
  for the vast majority of viewers, but if you post it to an audience where
  photosensitivity is a real concern, say so in the caption — or thin out the
  `HEAVY` set in `reel2.html` and re-render.
- **First comment:** put the TestFlight link there as well as the bio. People
  screenshot comments.
- The cuts sit on a **120 BPM** grid, so most gym audio lines up without nudging.
