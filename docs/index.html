LEVL · for iPhone

Your gym log, played as a game.

Coming soon to the App Store

Every set you log becomes XP, moves six stats and builds toward a level and a rank. Duel a friend, post a daily Check In, and spend what you earn on new looks for your character.

What's inside

  • Train. Log lifts and cardio, run Workout Days with a rest timer, and see your numbers over time.
  • Your player. A 3D character shaped by six stats: strength, power, endurance, vitality, mobility and discipline. Ranks run from Bronze to Grandmaster.
  • Compete. Challenge a friend to a duel, scored by our server from the workouts you log, or practise against simulated challengers.
  • Check In. Two photos at once, what you're training and you, shared with friends or everyone.
  • The Forge. Packs, upgrades and a Pass, all paid for with coins and XP you earn. No real-money purchases.

Play as a guest, or create an account to keep your progress in the cloud and train with friends.
