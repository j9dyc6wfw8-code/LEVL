Privacy Policy

This policy explains what LEVL collects, why, and what control you have. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

Who we are

LEVL is an iOS training app. The data controller is the LEVL developer. For any privacy question, or to exercise any right below, contact Levlup18@gmail.com.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, LEVL reads activity data to show alongside your training. This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded to our servers, never attached to a Check In, and never used to change your XP, stats or rank. You can disconnect it at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy → Health.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data. To anyone. Ever.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and there is no advertising in LEVL.
  • We do not use Apple Health data for anything except showing it back to you.
  • We do not share your email address with other users.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase — database, authentication and photo storage, hosted in the EU (eu-central-1).
  • Expo — delivers push notifications.
  • Apple — App Store, and Sign in with Apple if you use it.

Who can see what

Your training log is private by default. A Check In is visible to the audience you choose when posting — friends, or public. Your level, rank, username and display name appear on leaderboards and to friends. Your email address is never shown to another user.

Your rights

  • See your data — it is in the app; the backup code exports it in full.
  • Correct it — every logged set can be edited or deleted.
  • Delete everything — Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, training history, photographs, friendships and duels from our servers. It cannot be undone.
  • Object or restrict — contact us.
  • Complain — to your local data protection authority.

Under UK/EU GDPR our lawful basis is performance of a contract (running the app you asked for) and legitimate interest (keeping it working and free of abuse).

Children

LEVL is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
