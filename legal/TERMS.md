# LEVL — Terms of Use

**Last updated: 18 August 2026**

By creating an account you agree to these terms. They exist mostly to say two
things: be decent to other people, and do not cheat.

## Your account

You must be 13 or older. Keep your login details to yourself; you are
responsible for what happens under your account. One account per person.

## Acceptable use — the rules that get enforced

There is **zero tolerance** for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. You
may not post, send or upload:

- Sexual content, nudity, or anything sexualising a minor
- Harassment, bullying, threats, or targeting an individual
- Hate speech, or content attacking people for who they are
- Violence, gore, or promotion of self-harm or disordered eating
- Illegal content, or the promotion of illegal drugs including anabolic steroids
- Spam, scams, or impersonation of another person
- Anyone else's photograph without their permission

**Filtering.** Captions, comments, usernames and display names are checked
against a blocklist as you post them. Anything that matches is refused outright.

**Reporting.** Every Check In and comment can be reported from the ⋯ menu, and
any user can be blocked. Once three different people report the same post or
comment it is hidden automatically, straight away, before anyone has reviewed it.

**Review.** Reports are reviewed and acted on **within 24 hours**. Content that
breaks these rules is removed and the account may be suspended or terminated
without notice or refund.

## Fair play

LEVL is competitive. Do not:

- Log training you did not do, in order to gain XP, rank or win duels
- Modify, reverse-engineer or automate the app to alter your scores
- Use another person's account or data as your own

We may correct, reset or remove scores, ranks and rewards obtained this way, and
may terminate accounts that do it repeatedly.

## Health disclaimer

**LEVL is not medical advice.** It records training you choose to do and
estimates figures such as one-rep maxes from formulas that are approximations,
not measurements. Do not treat any number in this app as a safe weight to lift.
Training carries risk of injury. Consult a qualified professional before
starting a programme, and stop if something hurts.
