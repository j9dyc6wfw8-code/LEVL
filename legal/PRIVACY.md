# LEVL — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 18 August 2026**

This policy explains what LEVL collects, why, and what control you have. It is
written to be read, not to be survived.

## Who we are

LEVL is an iOS training app. The data controller is the LEVL developer. For any
privacy question, or to exercise any right below, contact **[YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]**.

## What we collect

**You give us this directly**

| Data | Why | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | To create and recover your account | Until you delete your account |
| Display name and username | Shown to friends and on leaderboards | Until you delete your account |
| Training log — exercises, weights, reps, effort, cardio, dates | The core function of the app | Until you delete it or your account |
| Bodyweight, height, age, sex, activity level, experience | To sanity-check implausible lifts and give context to stats | Until you delete your account |
| Check In photographs and captions | The social feed, if you use it | Until you delete the Check In or your account |
| Bug reports you submit | To fix the bug | Until you delete your account |

**Collected automatically**

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Device push token | To send the notifications you switch on |
| Timezone | To send reminders at the right local time |
| Approximate app usage events | To find crashes and see which screens fail |

**Apple Health**

If you connect Apple Health, LEVL reads activity data to show alongside your
training. **This data never leaves your device.** It is not uploaded to our
servers, never attached to a Check In, and never used to change your XP, stats
or rank. You can disconnect it at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy → Health.

## What we never do

- We do not sell your data. To anyone. Ever.
- We do not use your data for advertising, and there is no advertising in LEVL.
- We do not use Apple Health data for anything except showing it back to you.
- We do not share your email address with other users.

## Who processes it for us

- **Supabase** — database, authentication and photo storage, hosted in the EU
  (eu-central-1).
- **Expo** — delivers push notifications.
- **Apple** — App Store, and sign in with Apple if you use it.

## Who can see what

Your training log is private by default. A Check In is visible to the audience
you choose when posting — friends, or public. Your level, rank, username and
display name appear on leaderboards and to friends. Your email address is never
shown to another user.

## Your rights

You can, at any time:

- **See your data** — it is in the app; the backup code exports it in full.
- **Correct it** — every logged set can be edited or deleted.
- **Delete everything** — Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes
  your account, training history, photographs, friendships and duels from our
  servers. It cannot be undone.
- **Object or restrict** — contact us.
- **Complain** — to your local data protection authority.

Under UK/EU GDPR our lawful basis is performance of a contract (running the app
you asked for) and legitimate interest (keeping it working and free of abuse).

## Children

LEVL is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we
will remove it.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially we will say so in the app before the change
takes effect.
